Key Letters Guide: How to Sign A, B, D, and E

For highest recognition accuracy, position your hand clearly within the camera frame facing forward:

A
Letter 'A' (Fist)
Make a closed fist with all four fingers curled tightly. Keep your thumb pointing straight upward resting alongside the index finger.
Tip: Keep thumb resting alongside index finger (distinct from Thumbs Up for 'Done').
B
Letter 'B' (Flat Upright)
Hold all four fingers pointing straight UP pressed together, with your thumb folded across your palm.
Tip: Fold thumb across palm (distinct from open-hand 'Hello' where thumb is open).
D
Letter 'D' (Pointer Up)
Point your index finger straight UP. Touch the tips of your middle finger and thumb together to form an 'O' circle, with ring and pinky fingers curled.
Tip: Keep index finger vertical with thumb touching middle fingertip.
E
Letter 'E' (Claw Curl)
Curl all four fingers downward so their fingertips touch the top of your thumb tucked horizontally underneath them.
Tip: Tuck thumb horizontally flat beneath the four curled fingertips.
